## Purpose

[Alberta Mountain-aholics](https://www.facebook.com/groups/1409264842848628) (Hiking • Scrambling • Climbing • Mountaineering) is a **private** Facebook group to meet hikers, scramblers, climbers, and mountaineers through an environment 
where members can connect, share experiences, and build lasting friendships. 

- The group caters to those with an intermediate to advanced skill level in either hiking or climbing. 
- The group is not dedicated to finding activity partners, but the community is tight-knit and people use the group to coordinate activities.
- The group is used to post about trail conditions, scrambling and climbing routes, to find partners for avalanche and crevasse rescue courses, etc.
- Beyond the mountains, members also organize group gatherings, pub nights, and other social get-togethers, and are encouraged to host their own events.
- The group is a place where you interact with people first (share tips, ask for information, etc.) to build connections, and then find hiking/scrambling/climbing/mountaineering partners as a result of the interaction.
