These groups are where you find ideas for your next trip, ask about a route, and hear what a trail is like this week rather than last year.
For the detailed write-ups, browse the [trip reports by objective](/hiking-scrambling-beta/trip-reports), which index every report the major blogs have published, grouped by mountain.
Looking for people to hike with instead? See the [Facebook groups for finding partners](/meetup-groups/facebook).

## General hiking and scrambling

- [Hike Alberta](https://www.facebook.com/groups/1644376389112687) is probably the most popular Facebook group when it comes to looking for inspiration, pictures, and practical information.
  It is a bit of a monster in terms of volume of posts, and the approach is to give a little bit of everything for everyone.
- [Wild Canadian Rocky Mountain Hikers](https://www.facebook.com/groups/1907088892741238) is a popular group for advanced hikers and scramblers. The moderation team is trying their best to promote best practices and knowledge.
- [Scrambling in the Canadian Rockies](https://www.facebook.com/groups/2251181818/) is a popular group among [scramblers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scrambling). The Scrambling in the Canadian Rockies group is for sharing trip reports and conditions, finding partners, and seeking advice related to scrambling in the Canadian Rockies.

::tip
[Friends of Kananaskis Country](https://kananaskis.org/do-your-research/) makes a useful distinction between them:
a focused group like Scrambling in the Canadian Rockies is mostly posts with information you can plan from,
while a large general-interest group like Hike Alberta is better for ideas than for research.
Confirm anything that matters against the [official trail conditions](/weather-trail-conditions/trail-conditions).
::

## Backpacking and multi-day trips

- [Backpacking in BC (Overnight and Multi-Day Hiking)](https://www.facebook.com/groups/backpackinginbc) covers overnight and multi-day hikes in BC, which includes the Rockies on the BC side of the Divide: Yoho, Kootenay, Mount Assiniboine and Mount Robson.
- [Great Divide Trail Hikers](https://www.facebook.com/groups/GDThikers/) is the hikers' forum the [Great Divide Trail Association](https://greatdividetrail.com/) points to, for hikers past, present and future to share wisdom, thoughts and plans for day, section or thru-hikes on the GDT.

## Single destinations

Each of these covers one destination only, which keeps the posts on topic: permits and reservations, current conditions, and trip reports.

- [Lake O'Hara Hiking and Camping Planning, Updates, and Trip Reports](https://www.facebook.com/groups/lakeohara)
- [Mount Assiniboine Park Planning, Updates, and Trip Reports](https://www.facebook.com/groups/mountassiniboine)
- [Rockwall Trail Planning, Updates, and Trip Reports](https://www.facebook.com/groups/rockwalltrail)
- [Mt. Temple Scramble Q&A/Info](https://www.facebook.com/groups/247447826919134), for one of the most climbed scrambles in the Rockies.
