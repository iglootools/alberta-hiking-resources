## 📣 Call for Help

[Sami](/hike-organizers/sami) — the original creator of this site — is full of hiking stoke but not quite full of all the knowledge in the Rockies 🏔️.

This site isn't meant to be a one-hiker show… it's an open trailhead for a shared effort to make info better, richer, and more helpful for everyone 📚.

👋 Wanna Lend a Boot?

You're more than welcome to contribute if any of this sounds like you:
- 🌐 You know of great hiking groups we should highlight, or can help flesh out info on the ones already listed 📝
- 🥾 You're a hike organizer and want to share your vibe, style, and what folks should expect on your adventures 💬
- 🔧 You've got trail wisdom to drop — whether it's safety tips, gear recs, or “I learned this the hard way” kind of knowledge 🧠

Whether it's a quick fix, a full section, or just a friendly pointer to better info — every little bit helps 🫶🌲
Let's build this trail guide together! 🛠️🗺️✨

### ✏️ Ways to pitch in

For a quick fix — a typo, a dead link, a group we've missed — use the **'Edit this page'** link to the right of each page. It drops you straight into [GitHub](https://github.com/iglootools/alberta-hiking-resources) and walks you through proposing the change. No setup, and you don't need to know how any of this is built 🙌

For something meatier — a whole new page, or reshuffling a section — you can open the entire site in your browser and watch your edits appear live as you type:

- 🛠️ **[Open in GitHub Codespaces](https://codespaces.new/iglootools/alberta-hiking-resources)** — a full development environment in your browser, nothing installed on your machine. Editor on one side, the site running on the other. Needs a GitHub account.

Prefer to work on your own machine? The [development setup guide](https://github.com/iglootools/alberta-hiking-resources/blob/main/docs/setup-development-environment.md) has you covered 💻

Not sure which to pick, or it all sounds a bit much? Open an [issue](https://github.com/iglootools/alberta-hiking-resources/issues) and just tell us what you know — someone will help get it onto the site 🫂
