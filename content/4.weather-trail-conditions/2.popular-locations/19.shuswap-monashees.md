[trip reports by objective](/hiking-scrambling-beta/trip-reports/shuswap-monashees) for this area, or
find [somewhere to sleep](/accommodation/shuswap-monashees).

Between the dry Okanagan and the wet Selkirks, and getting a bit of both: lake-country heat and
smoke in summer, real snow in the Monashees. Monashee Park is a long gravel drive past
Cherryville, so check the forecast for where you will be, not where you leave the pavement.
Lumby sits between this region and the Okanagan, so it is on both pages.
