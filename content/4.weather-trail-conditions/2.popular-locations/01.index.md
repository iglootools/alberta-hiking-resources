Each area below has its own page of forecast, air quality, and webcam links, grouped by the
towns you would actually check before driving out. These are the same regions the
[trip reports](/hiking-scrambling-beta/trip-reports) and [Accommodation](/accommodation) pages
use, so you can line up the forecast with a place to sleep and with who has been there.

A town that is useful from two directions is listed under both regions rather than under
whichever came first — Saskatchewan River Crossing serves the Icefields Parkway and David
Thompson Country equally, and Golden serves Yoho, Rogers Pass, and the Purcells.
