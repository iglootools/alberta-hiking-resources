Working out where to go? Browse the
[trip reports by objective](/hiking-scrambling-beta/trip-reports/icefields-parkway) for this area, and
check the [forecast](/weather-trail-conditions/popular-locations/icefields-parkway) before you commit.

The Parkway runs 230 km with no town on it. Campgrounds and hostels are strung along its
length, roughly an hour apart, and most are basic — no services, and several are
first-come, first-served.

**Banff National Park (southern half)** — [camping & booking](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff/activ/camping)

- **Reservable** — Rampart Creek, Silverhorn Creek
- **First-come, first-served** — Mosquito Creek, Waterfowl Lakes

**Jasper National Park (northern half)** — [camping & booking](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/jasper/activ/passez-stay/camping)

- **First-come, first-served** — Wilcox, Columbia Icefield (tents) and the Icefields Centre RV lot, Jonas Creek, Honeymoon Lake, Mount Kerkeslin

**Hostels** — four wilderness hostels, spaced along the highway and mostly off-grid

- [HI Mosquito Creek](https://hihostels.ca/en/destinations/hi-mosquito-creek) — 24 km north of Lake Louise
- [HI Rampart Creek](https://hihostels.ca/en/destinations/hi-rampart-creek) — near Saskatchewan River Crossing
- [HI Hilda Creek](https://hihostels.ca/en/destinations/hi-hilda-creek) — group rental only, 8 km south of the Icefields Centre
- [HI Beauty Creek](https://hihostels.ca/en/destinations/hi-beauty-creek) — north of the Icefield, on the Jasper side

**Alpine Club of Canada huts**

Huts book through the [Alpine Club of Canada](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/huts/) on a rolling window — six months ahead for members, three for everyone else. The first five below are the Wapta Icefield chain, the classic multi-day ski
traverse; all are glacier travel, not hikes.

- [Bow Hut](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/bow-hut/) — from Bow Lake, and the usual first night on the Wapta
- [Peyto Hut](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/peter-and-catharine-whyte-peyto-hut/) (Peter and Catharine Whyte) — from Bow Lake
- [Balfour Hut](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/rob-ritchie-balfour-hut/) (Rob Ritchie) — from Bow Lake
- [Mt. Daly Hut](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/scott-duncan-hut/) (Scott Duncan) — the last hut on the traverse, above Sherbrooke Lake
- [Yoho Peak Hut](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/louise-and-richard-guy-hut/) (Louise and Richard Guy) — on the Wapta's Yoho side
- [Mt. Alberta Hut](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/lloyd-mackay-mt-alberta-hut/) (Lloyd MacKay) — from the Stutfield Glacier viewpoint; a serious mountaineering approach

Either end has more choice: [Banff National Park](/accommodation/banff) to the south,
[Jasper and Mount Robson](/accommodation/jasper-robson) to the north. Saskatchewan River
Crossing, the one fuel stop on the highway, is also the gateway to
[David Thompson Country](/accommodation/david-thompson) on Highway 11.
