A ferry away from everything else here, and worth the crossing for Strathcona. Campgrounds
book through [BC Parks](https://camping.bcparks.ca/).

**Campgrounds** — BC Parks

- [Strathcona Park](https://bcparks.ca/strathcona-park/) — Buttle Lake and Ralph River, the two serviced campgrounds, plus backcountry camping on the Forbidden Plateau
- [Elk Falls](https://bcparks.ca/elk-falls-park/) — just outside Campbell River, handy for a late arrival
- [Pacific Rim National Park Reserve](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/pacificrim/activ/camping) — Green Point, on the west coast at Tofino

**Hostels**

- [HI Cumberland](https://hihostels.ca/en/destinations/hi-cumberland) — in the Comox Valley, closest to Strathcona
- [HI Tofino](https://hihostels.ca/en/destinations/hi-tofino) — on the west coast

**Alpine Club of Canada huts**

Huts book through the [Alpine Club of Canada](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/huts/) on a rolling window — six months ahead for members, three for everyone else.

- [Hišimy̓awiƛ](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/hisimy%cc%93awi%c6%9b-hi-shim-ya-wit/) — the Vancouver Island Section hut, on the Forbidden Plateau in Strathcona Park

**Recreation sites**

Crown land camping on the Island is real but awkwardly placed: the recreation sites cluster
around the Sayward Forest canoe route, Cowichan Lake and the west-coast inlets rather than
around Strathcona, which is where the hiking is. First come, first served. See
[how it works](/accommodation/how-booking-works#camping-on-bc-crown-land) for the rules.

- [Cougar Creek](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC0471) — 65 sites on Tlupana Inlet near Gold River, $15, gated for the
  season in mid-September. Gold River is the Strathcona back door.
- [Muchalat Lake](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC0258) — 40 sites, also out of Gold River.
- [Maple Grove](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC0206), [Nixon Creek](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC0211) and [Pine Point](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC0205) — the
  Cowichan Lake trio, 60 to 83 sites each, fee.
- [Pacific Yew](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC261703) and [Gosling Lake](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC0163) — on the Sayward Forest canoe route
  out of Campbell River, the newer and the quieter.
- [Elk Bay](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC5810) and [Pye Beach](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC0297) — free, and two of the few with ocean access.

::warning
**Much of the Island is not Crown land.** Roughly 550,000 hectares between Victoria and Campbell
River is privately owned managed forest administered by
[Mosaic Forest Management](https://www.mosaicforests.com/access), which controls it with more
than twenty gates. Access to Mount Arrowsmith, Century Sam and other well-known objectives runs
through them, and is often limited to weekend daylight hours. Mosaic also runs its own
[campgrounds](https://www.mosaicforests.com/camping). None of the Crown land rules above apply
behind those gates — check the gate schedule before driving out, because a closed one ends the
day.
::
