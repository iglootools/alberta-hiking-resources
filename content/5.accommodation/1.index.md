Want to sleep close to the trailhead? This page covers the three main ways to do it — **campgrounds**, **hostels**, and **backcountry huts** — plus how the booking systems work and which spots are reservable versus first-come, first-served.

Start with [How Booking Works](/accommodation/how-booking-works) for the reservation systems and launch dates, then pick your area below.

**These are all frontcountry campgrounds** — car-accessible, drive-up sites you use as a basecamp, not backcountry sites you hike into. (For huts you hike to, see [Backcountry Huts](/accommodation/backcountry-huts).)

Each area below has its own page, using the same regions as the [trip reports](/hiking-scrambling-beta/trip-reports) and the [Popular Locations](/weather-trail-conditions/popular-locations) weather page, so a forecast, a bed, and beta for the same area are one path segment apart. Within each area, the important campgrounds are grouped by how you get a spot:

- **Reservable** — book ahead through the operator's reservation system (see [How Booking Works](/accommodation/how-booking-works)).
- **First-come, first-served (FCFS)** — no reservations; you claim a site in person, so arrive early on summer weekends.

::warning
**Always confirm before you go.** Reservable-vs-FCFS status, operating seasons, and closures change from year to year, and wildfires and floods can shut sites down at short notice. Treat this as a starting point and check the official link for current details.
::
