**Glacier National Park, BC (Rogers Pass)** — [camping & booking](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/glacier/activ/passez-stay/camping)

- **Reservable** — Loop Brook; Illecillewaet *(roughly half reservable, half FCFS)*
- **Mount Sir Donald** is closed until further notice.

**Mount Revelstoke National Park** — [camping & booking](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/revelstoke/activ/passez-stay/camping)

- **Reservable** — Snowforest (the park's only frontcountry campground; hard-sided units, and walk-in tent sites have been temporarily unavailable — verify)

**Near Revelstoke (BC Parks)** — [Martha Creek](https://bcparks.ca/martha-creek-park/) and [Blanket Creek](https://bcparks.ca/blanket-creek-park/), both a mix of reservable and FCFS sites.

**Golden, BC** — the practical basecamp for Golden is the Yoho National Park campgrounds just east of town (see [Banff, Lake Louise, and Yoho](/accommodation/yoho)).

- **Hostels** — none in Rogers Pass, Revelstoke, or Golden. The nearest, [HI Yoho / Whiskey Jack](https://hihostels.ca/en/destinations/hi-yoho-national-park), is temporarily closed.

**Alpine Club of Canada huts** — more than any other region here

Huts book through the [Alpine Club of Canada](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/huts/) on a rolling window — six months ahead for members, three for everyone else.

- [Illecillewaet Hut](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/a-o-wheeler-hut/) (A. O. Wheeler) — at the Illecillewaet campground, road-accessible and the easiest of these by far
- [Asulkan Cabin](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/asulkan-cabin/) — up the Asulkan valley from the same trailhead
- [Sapphire Col Hut](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/sapphire-col-hut/) — a col bivouac hut above the Asulkan
- [Glacier Circle Cabin](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/glacier-circle-cabin/) — remote, behind the Illecillewaet névé
- [Fairy Meadow Hut](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/bill-putnam-fairy-meadow-hut/) (Bill Putnam) — Adamant Range, helicopter access from Lake Kinbasket; winter weeks are allocated by [lottery](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/bill-putnam-fairy-meadow-hut-lottery/)
- [Great Cairn Hut](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/ben-ferris-great-cairn-hut/) (Ben Ferris) — Sir Sandford, also by helicopter
