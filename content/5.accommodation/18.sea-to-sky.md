A different range and a different climate: wetter, lower snowlines in winter, and far busier
on summer weekends. Campgrounds here book months out through
[BC Parks](https://camping.bcparks.ca/).

**Campgrounds** — BC Parks

- [Garibaldi Provincial Park](https://bcparks.ca/garibaldi-park/) — backcountry only, by permit; the Black Tusk, Panorama Ridge and Elfin Lakes areas all sit inside it
- [Alice Lake](https://bcparks.ca/alice-lake-park/) — the main serviced campground near Squamish
- [Porteau Cove](https://bcparks.ca/porteau-cove-park/) — on the water between Vancouver and Squamish
- [Nairn Falls](https://bcparks.ca/nairn-falls-park/) — just south of Pemberton, and the closest to Joffre Lakes

**Hostels**

- [HI Whistler](https://hihostels.ca/en/destinations/hi-whistler) — in Whistler, built as athlete housing for 2010
- [HI Vancouver Downtown](https://hihostels.ca/en/destinations/hi-vancouver-downtown)
- [HI Vancouver Jericho Beach](https://hihostels.ca/en/destinations/hi-vancouver-jericho-beach) — seasonal

**Alpine Club of Canada huts**

Huts book through the [Alpine Club of Canada](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/huts/) on a rolling window — six months ahead for members, three for everyone else.

- [Kees and Claire Memorial Hut](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/huts/) — Russet Lake, on the Spearhead Traverse above Whistler *(the club lists it, but its own page is currently broken)*
- [Wendy Thompson Hut](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/wendy-thompson-hut/) — Marriott Basin, north of Pemberton
- [Jim Haberl Hut](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/jim-haberl-hut/) — Serratus–Dione col, Tantalus Range
- [Tantalus Hut](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/tantalus-hut/) — Lake Lovely Water, Tantalus Range

**Recreation sites**

The alternative to the provincial parks here, and often the only thing close to a trailhead.
First come, first served unless noted; fee sites are cash to the operator. See
[how it works](/accommodation/how-booking-works#camping-on-bc-crown-land) for the rules and the Forest Service road caveats.

- [Tenquille Lake – Hawint](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC0237) — the exception to everything: **reservation only**,
  $25 a site for tenting or $25 a person for the cabin, booked through the Pemberton Wildlife
  Association. Co-managed by Recreation Sites and Trails BC with the Lil̓wat and N'Quatqua
  nations. An alpine lake you hike to, not drive to.
- [Cal-cheak](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC0127) — 63 sites between Callaghan Creek and the Cheakamus, $18, the
  practical Whistler basecamp when the park campgrounds are full.
- [Cat Lake](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC0105) — 48 walk-in sites near Squamish, $18, and a standing bear problem:
  use the caches and leave nothing out.
- [Chek Canyon](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC32295) — free, 32 sites at a climbing area with about 100 routes; steep
  access road, all-wheel drive recommended. Picnic tables, fire rings, outhouses and bear caches
  throughout.
- [Strawberry Point](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC0221), [Lizzie Bay](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC0225) and [Driftwood Bay](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC0289) —
  the Lillooet Lake string on the In-SHUCK-ch Forest Service Road, and
  [Lizzie Lake](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC0233) further up it, which is the way into the Stein Divide.
- [Owl Creek](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC0219) — fifteen sites near Pemberton.

Check each listing before committing: wildfire closures on the In-SHUCK-ch road recur, and
several of these sites carry standing access advisories.
