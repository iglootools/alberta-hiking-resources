**Banff National Park** — [camping & booking](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff/activ/camping)

- **Reservable** — Tunnel Mountain (Village I & II and Trailer Court), Two Jack Lakeside, Two Jack Main (Banff townsite); Johnston Canyon, Castle Mountain, Protection Mountain (Bow Valley Parkway); Lake Louise (tent & trailer). All Banff and Lake Louise frontcountry campgrounds are reservable.

**Hostels**

- [HI Banff Alpine Centre](https://hihostels.ca/en/destinations/hi-banff) — Tunnel Mountain, above the townsite
- [HI Lake Louise Alpine Centre](https://hihostels.ca/en/destinations/hi-lake-louise) — run jointly with the [Alpine Club of Canada](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/hi-lake-louise-alpine-centre/)
- [HI Castle Mountain Wilderness Hostel](https://hihostels.ca/en/destinations/hi-castle-mountain) — Bow Valley Parkway, basic and off-grid

**Alpine Club of Canada huts**

Huts book through the [Alpine Club of Canada](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/huts/) on a rolling window — six months ahead for members, three for everyone else.

- [Castle Mountain Cabin](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/castle-mountain-hut/) — Castle Junction
- [Valley of the Ten Peaks Hut](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/neil-colgan-hut/) (Neil Colgan) — above Moraine Lake, and the highest permanent structure in Canada; a mountaineering approach, not a hike
- [Shadow Lake Lodge](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/shadow-lake-lodge/) — catered, reached on foot or ski from Redearth Creek

North of Lake Louise the park continues up the [Icefields Parkway](/accommodation/icefields-parkway), which has its own campgrounds and hostels.
