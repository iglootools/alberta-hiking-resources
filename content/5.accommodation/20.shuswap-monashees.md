[trip reports by objective](/hiking-scrambling-beta/trip-reports/shuswap-monashees) for this area, and

Lake country on the Shuswap side, and a long gravel road into the Monashees on the other:
Monashee Park is about 62 km past Cherryville, the last of it on active logging roads. The
lakeside campgrounds book through [BC Parks](https://camping.bcparks.ca/) and are full from
July to Labour Day.

**Campgrounds** — BC Parks

- [Shuswap Lake](https://bcparks.ca/shuswap-lake-park/) — at Scotch Creek on the north shore, 19 km from the highway at Squilax, and the one that fills first
- [Herald](https://bcparks.ca/herald-park/) — on the Salmon Arm of the lake near Tappen, with the Margaret Falls trail in the park
- [Mabel Lake](https://bcparks.ca/mabel-lake-park/) — two campgrounds, Trinity and Monashee, 37 km north-east of Lumby
- [Monashee](https://bcparks.ca/monashee-park/) — backcountry camping at Spectrum Lake, 6 km from the trailhead, and Peters Lake, reached from Cherryville by the Sugar Lake and Spectrum Creek forest service roads; low-clearance vehicles may struggle at the log sort

**Hostels** — Hostelling International has none in the Shuswap or the Monashees.

**Huts** — the Alpine Club of Canada has none in the Monashees.

**Recreation sites**

First come, first served, with no reservations — see
[how it works](/accommodation/how-booking-works#camping-on-bc-crown-land) for the rules. The fee
sites charge $18 a night from May to September.

- [Noisy Creek](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC1750) — fee site, 60 sites at the north end of Mabel Lake, with a sandy beach and a 45-minute trail up Noisy Creek
- [Hidden Lake](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC1742) — fee site, 48 sites east of Enderby
- [Sugar Lake 2 Mile](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC1502) and [Sugar Creek](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC1506) — fee sites, 62 and 23 sites on Sugar Lake, on the way to Monashee Park; Sugar Creek is no place for a long trailer
- [Cascade Falls](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC1863) — six free tent sites on Mabel Lake, rough access
- [Frog Falls](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC1566) — five free sites on Wap Creek near Three Valley Gap, with a 1 km loop to the falls
