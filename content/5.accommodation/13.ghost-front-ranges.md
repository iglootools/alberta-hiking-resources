Mostly public land rather than park, so this is the one region where the usual answer is
random camping rather than a booked site. Access is by unpaved road, and the Ghost's are
rough enough that a low car is a real limitation.

**Public land** — the Ghost and the Waiparous share a
[public land use zone](https://www.alberta.ca/public-land-use-zones); random camping is
permitted under its rules, with no facilities and no booking. Read the zone's own conditions
before going — camping is restricted in some areas and seasons, and a pass is required.

**Ghost Reservoir** — Alberta Parks

- **Reservable** — [Ghost Reservoir](https://www.albertaparks.ca/parks/kananaskis/ghost-reservoir-pra/information-facilities/camping/ghost-reservoir/), the nearest serviced campground, on the reservoir east of the Ghost River road.

**Ya Ha Tinda** — the Bighorn campground sits by the Red Deer River at the end of the Ya Ha
Tinda road from Sundre, on the boundary of Banff National Park. It is first-come,
first-served, and popular with horse parties; there is no online booking.

- **Hostels and huts** — none. Neither Hostelling International nor the Alpine Club of Canada has a property here, which fits a region where the usual bed is your own tent on public land.

For a serviced site with facilities, [Bow Valley Provincial Park](/accommodation/kananaskis-canmore)
at Seebe is about forty minutes south, and the nearest hostel is
[HI Kananaskis](https://hihostels.ca/en/destinations/hi-kananaskis) down Highway 40.
