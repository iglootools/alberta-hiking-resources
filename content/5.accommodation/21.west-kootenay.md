The southern Selkirks, and the densest concentration of Alpine Club huts outside Rogers Pass.
Kokanee Glacier Provincial Park holds three of them, each on its own side of the park.

**Campgrounds** — BC Parks

- [Kokanee Creek](https://bcparks.ca/kokanee-creek-park/) — on Kootenay Lake, the main serviced campground and the road up to the Gibson Lake trailhead
- [Kokanee Glacier](https://bcparks.ca/kokanee-glacier-park/) — the park itself, with backcountry camping alongside the cabins

**Hostels**

- [HI Nelson](https://hihostels.ca/en/destinations/hi-nelson) — in town, and the usual staging point

**Alpine Club of Canada huts**

Huts book through the [Alpine Club of Canada](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/huts/) on a rolling window — six months ahead for members, three for everyone else.

- [Kokanee Glacier Cabin](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/kokanee-glacier-cabin/) — from the Gibson Lake trailhead; winter weeks are allocated by [lottery](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/kokanee-glacier-cabin-lottery/)
- [Silver Spray Cabin](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/silver-spray-cabin/) — from the Woodbury–Fletcher forest service road, on the park's east side
- [Woodbury Cabin](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/woodbury-cabin/) — from the Woodbury trailhead, the smallest of the three

**Recreation sites**

Crown land camping, first come, first served, most of it free — see
[how it works](/accommodation/how-booking-works#camping-on-bc-crown-land) for the rules and the road caveats.

- [Bannock Point](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC5077) — free, and the pick of them: 34 walk-in sites on Slocan Lake
  reached by a 700 m moderately steep trail, rebuilt in 2024 with 12 sites up top and 22 down
  by the water. On the Valhalla side of the lake rather than the Kokanee side.
- [Six Mile Lakes](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC2123) — four free sites at three connected sub-alpine lakes south of
  Nelson, with a 3 km nature trail from just south of the site.
- [Boundary Lake](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC2097) — eleven free sites at a sub-alpine lake above Creston, forested
  and popular with ATVs.
- [Halfway River Hotsprings](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC2279) — fee site north of Nakusp, twenty sites, and the
  reason most people go.
- [Little Slocan](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC5352) and [Stevens Creek](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC2198) — free valley-bottom sites for a
  night on the way through.

Fees in the Kootenay–Boundary district start on 1 April, and there are no reservations anywhere
in it — everything is first come.
