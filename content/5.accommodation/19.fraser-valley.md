Working out where to go? Browse the
[trip reports by objective](/hiking-scrambling-beta/trip-reports/fraser-valley) for this area, and
check the [forecast](/weather-trail-conditions/popular-locations/fraser-valley) before you commit.

Day-trip range from Vancouver rather than from Calgary, and the wettest of the regions here.
Most campgrounds book through [BC Parks](https://camping.bcparks.ca/).

**Campgrounds** — BC Parks

- [Cultus Lake](https://bcparks.ca/cultus-lake-park/) — large and busy, west of Chilliwack
- [Skagit Valley](https://bcparks.ca/skagit-valley-park/) — up the Silver–Skagit road from Hope, and the quiet way into this corner
- [E.C. Manning](https://bcparks.ca/ec-manning-park/) — Lightning Lake and the smaller sites, on Highway 3
- [Golden Ears](https://bcparks.ca/golden-ears-park/) — Alouette Lake, north of Maple Ridge
- [Sasquatch](https://bcparks.ca/sasquatch-park/) — Harrison Lake, north-east of Chilliwack

Chilliwack Lake Provincial Park, the base for the Cheam Range and the Chilliwack valley
peaks, also has a campground; BC Parks does not currently publish a stable page for it, so
check [camping.bcparks.ca](https://camping.bcparks.ca/) directly.

**Hostels and huts** — none. Neither Hostelling International nor the Alpine Club of Canada
has a property in the valley; the nearest are in
[Sea to Sky and the Coast Mountains](/accommodation/sea-to-sky).

**Recreation sites**

The Chilliwack River valley is lined with them, which makes this the easiest region in BC to
camp at the foot of what you are climbing — the road to Chilliwack Lake is also the road to
Cheam, Slesse and the Border Peaks. First come, first served, cash only. See
[how it works](/accommodation/how-booking-works#camping-on-bc-crown-land) for the rules.

- [Tamihi Creek](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC0002) — the big one, 116 sites in three parts (East, West, and a day-use
  staging area where overnight camping is not allowed), $17, two-wheel-drive accessible, on the
  Trans Canada Trail.
- [Thurston Meadows](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC3038) — 76 sites in an open meadow straight off the Chilliwack Lake
  Road, $17, the RV-friendly one.
- [Riverside](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC0013) — fifteen sites on a tributary, $17, and the listing's own words are
  *"popular with hikers"*. Gates lock at 11 pm.
- [Allison Pool](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC0019) — seven sites, $17, gravel access and not for large RVs.
- [Bear Creek](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC0066), [Cascade Peninsula](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC0087) and [Cogburn Beach](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC3113) —
  free sites on Harrison Lake, 25 to 40 sites each, further from the hiking.
- [Sloquet Hot Springs](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC0023) — twenty sites up the In-SHUCK-ch road, subject to the same
  wildfire advisories as the Lillooet Lake sites.

Reservations for the Chilliwack valley fee sites go through the operator at
[campchilliwackvalley.ca](https://campchilliwackvalley.ca), not through the province.
