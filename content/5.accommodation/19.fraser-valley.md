Day-trip range from Vancouver rather than from Calgary, and the wettest of the regions here.
Most campgrounds book through [BC Parks](https://camping.bcparks.ca/).

**Campgrounds** — BC Parks

- [Cultus Lake](https://bcparks.ca/cultus-lake-park/) — large and busy, west of Chilliwack
- [Skagit Valley](https://bcparks.ca/skagit-valley-park/) — up the Silver–Skagit road from Hope, and the quiet way into this corner
- [E.C. Manning](https://bcparks.ca/ec-manning-park/) — Lightning Lake and the smaller sites, on Highway 3
- [Golden Ears](https://bcparks.ca/golden-ears-park/) — Alouette Lake, north of Maple Ridge
- [Sasquatch](https://bcparks.ca/sasquatch-park/) — Harrison Lake, north-east of Chilliwack

Chilliwack Lake Provincial Park, the base for the Cheam Range and the Chilliwack valley
peaks, also has a campground; BC Parks does not currently publish a stable page for it, so
check [camping.bcparks.ca](https://camping.bcparks.ca/) directly.

**Hostels and huts** — none. Neither Hostelling International nor the Alpine Club of Canada
has a property in the valley; the nearest are in
[Sea to Sky and the Coast Mountains](/accommodation/sea-to-sky).
