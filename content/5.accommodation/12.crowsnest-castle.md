**Crowsnest Pass & Castle region** — Alberta Parks

- **Reservable** — [Chinook Lake](https://www.albertaparks.ca/parks/south/chinook-pra/information-facilities/camping/chinook/); [Beaver Mines Lake](https://www.albertaparks.ca/parks/south/castle-pp/information-facilities/camping/beaver-mines-lake/), [Castle Falls](https://www.albertaparks.ca/parks/south/castle-pp/information-facilities/camping/castle-falls/), and Castle River Bridge (Castle PP)
- **First-come, first-served** — Lynx Creek and the numbered designated camping areas ([Castle PP camping index](https://www.albertaparks.ca/parks/south/castle-pp/information-facilities/camping/))
- **Hostels and huts** — none. Neither Hostelling International nor the Alpine Club of Canada has a property in the Crowsnest or the Castle; the nearest hut is the [Cameron Lake Cabin](/accommodation/waterton) in Waterton.

**Public land camping**

North and east of the pass the land is public rather than park, and random camping is
permitted there. See
[how it works](/accommodation/how-booking-works#camping-on-alberta-public-land) for the pass
and the province-wide rules; what is specific to here:

- **[Livingstone PLUZ](https://www.alberta.ca/livingstone)** — the Forestry Trunk Road country
  north of Coleman, up the Oldman and the Livingstone Range. Random camping is permitted
  across most of it, **except in the Willow Creek area, where camping is confined to designated
  nodes** and campfires are permitted only in those nodes. The nodes are free and first-come,
  and still need the pass.
- **[Porcupine Hills PLUZ](https://www.alberta.ca/porcupine-hills)** — east towards Nanton.
  Random camping is permitted, 1 km back from any Provincial Recreation Area. The pass is
  required here specifically, even though the zone sits outside the main Eastern Slopes
  boundary.

The Castle itself is now **provincial park and wildland**, not public land, so none of the
above applies there. The numbered
[designated camping areas](https://www.albertaparks.ca/parks/south/castle-pp/information-facilities/camping/)
listed above are the former random camping areas, converted to rustic fee campgrounds with
vault toilets and fire rings. In **Castle Wildland Provincial Park** backcountry tent camping
is still allowed with no permit and no pass, 1 km from any road or facility — but vehicle
camping, and camping at staging areas and day-use lots, are prohibited.

Check the
[Interactive Recreation Map](https://esrd.maps.arcgis.com/apps/webappviewer/index.html?id=e5651574de1342d7ae8277ef415864be)
for the zone and node boundaries, and
[public land closures](https://www.alberta.ca/public-land-closures) before driving out.
