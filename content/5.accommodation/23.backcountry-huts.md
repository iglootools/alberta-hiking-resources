The Alpine Club of Canada operates a network of [backcountry huts](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/huts/)
through the Rockies, the Selkirks, and beyond. Booking is a rolling window: reservations open
**six months ahead for ACC members and three months ahead for non-members**, which is why
membership pays for itself if you use them.

A hut is not a hostel. Most have no power or running water, several need glacier travel or a
mountaineering approach to reach at all, and a few are allocated by lottery rather than
first-come. Read the individual hut page before booking one.

## By region

Each hut is listed on the page for the region it is reached from:

- [Kananaskis Country and Canmore](/accommodation/kananaskis-canmore) — Canmore Alpine Hostel (the club's national clubhouse), Elk Lakes Cabin
- [Banff National Park](/accommodation/banff) — Castle Mountain Cabin, Valley of the Ten Peaks Hut, Shadow Lake Lodge, HI Lake Louise Alpine Centre
- [Yoho National Park](/accommodation/yoho) — Lake O'Hara Hut, Little Yoho Valley Hut
- [Icefields Parkway](/accommodation/icefields-parkway) — the Wapta chain (Bow, Peyto, Balfour, Mt. Daly, Yoho Peak) and Mt. Alberta Hut
- [Jasper and Mount Robson](/accommodation/jasper-robson) — Fryatt Creek, Ramparts, Mt. Colin Centennial, Jacques Lake, Robson Pass
- [Waterton Lakes](/accommodation/waterton) — Cameron Lake Cabin
- [BC Rockies and the Purcells](/accommodation/bc-rockies-purcells) — Bugaboo Hut, Elk Lakes Cabin
- [Rogers Pass, the Selkirks, and Golden](/accommodation/rogers-pass-selkirks) — Illecillewaet, Asulkan, Sapphire Col, Glacier Circle, Fairy Meadow, Great Cairn
- [Sea to Sky and the Coast Mountains](/accommodation/sea-to-sky) — Kees and Claire, Wendy Thompson, Jim Haberl, Tantalus
- [West Kootenay](/accommodation/west-kootenay) — Kokanee Glacier, Silver Spray, Woodbury
- [Vancouver Island](/accommodation/vancouver-island) — Hišimy̓awiƛ

## Outside these regions

Two huts belong to eastern sections of the club, well beyond anything this site covers:
[Keene Farm Hut](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/keene-farm/) (Montreal Section) and the
[Wally Joyce Hut](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/wally-joyce-bon-echo-hut/) at Bon Echo
(Toronto Section).

Not every hut in the mountains is an ACC hut. The Naiset Huts and the Hind Hut at
[Mount Assiniboine](/accommodation/assiniboine) are BC Parks and lodge-managed, and are booked
separately.
