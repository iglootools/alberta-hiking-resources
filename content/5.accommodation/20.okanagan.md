Hot and dry, and the one region here where summer heat is more likely to shape a trip than
snow. Campgrounds book through [BC Parks](https://camping.bcparks.ca/) and fill early.

**Campgrounds** — BC Parks

- [Okanagan Lake](https://bcparks.ca/okanagan-lake-park/) — north of Summerland, on the lake
- [Cathedral Park](https://bcparks.ca/cathedral-park/) — core-area camping in the high country west of Keremeos, reached on foot or by the lodge's 4×4 shuttle
- [Kalamalka Lake](https://bcparks.ca/kalamalka-lake-park/) — day use and trails near Vernon
- [Wells Gray](https://bcparks.ca/wells-gray-park/) — north of Kamloops, and a long day from anywhere else on this page

**Hostels**

- [HI Kelowna](https://hihostels.ca/en/destinations/hi-kelowna)
- [HI Penticton](https://hihostels.ca/en/destinations/hi-penticton)

**Huts** — the Alpine Club of Canada has none in the Okanagan.

**Recreation sites**

There are more recreation sites here than in any other region on this list — around 200 with
camping — but it is worth being clear about what they are. Almost all sit on fishing lakes on
the plateau rather than at a trailhead, so they are a cheap bed and a place to swim, not a
basecamp under a peak. First come, first served, cash only. See
[how it works](/accommodation/how-booking-works#camping-on-bc-crown-land) for the rules.

The larger ones, all fee sites: [Lundbom Lake](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC1883) (102 sites, Merritt),
[Noisy Creek](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC1750) (60, Mabel Lake), [Duffy Lake](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC1545) (54, Kamloops),
[Paska Lake](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC1556) (49, Logan Lake), [Hidden Lake](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC1742) (48, Enderby),
[Evely](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC1596) (48, Vernon) and [Harmon Lake](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/resource/REC1715) (32, Merritt).

The full set is best browsed on the
[site and trail search](https://www.sitesandtrailsbc.ca/search) filtered to this area — with
this many, a list here would go stale faster than it would help. Wildfire closures are the thing
to check: sites west of Kelowna are still shut or partly shut following the 2023 McDougall Creek
fire.
