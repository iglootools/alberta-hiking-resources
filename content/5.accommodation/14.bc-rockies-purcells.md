Almost all BC Parks here, which book through a
[single reservation system](https://camping.bcparks.ca/) rather than park by park.

**Elk Valley (Fernie, Elkford)**

- [Mount Fernie](https://bcparks.ca/mount-fernie-park/) — the closest campground to Fernie and its trails, reservable
- [Elk Lakes](https://bcparks.ca/elk-lakes-park/) — walk-in and backcountry at the head of the valley, reached by a long gravel road from Elkford
- [Kikomun Creek](https://bcparks.ca/kikomun-creek-park/) — south towards Cranbrook, larger and warmer

**Columbia Valley (Invermere, Radium)**

- [Dry Gulch](https://bcparks.ca/dry-gulch-park/) — small, just south of Radium and handy for both the Purcells and Kootenay National Park
- [Whiteswan Lake](https://bcparks.ca/whiteswan-lake-park/) and [Top of the World](https://bcparks.ca/top-of-the-world-park/) — east into the Rockies on forest service roads; Top of the World is backcountry only

**The Bugaboos**

- [Bugaboo Provincial Park](https://bcparks.ca/bugaboo-park/) — the Applebee dome camping area and the Conrad Kain Hut, the latter booked through the [Alpine Club of Canada](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/huts/)

**Hostels**

- [HI Fernie Backpacker Hostel](https://hihostels.ca/en/destinations/hi-fernie) — in town, the only one in this region

**Alpine Club of Canada huts**

Huts book through the [Alpine Club of Canada](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/huts/) on a rolling window — six months ahead for members, three for everyone else.

- [Bugaboo Hut](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/conrad-kain-hut/) (Conrad Kain) — below the Bugaboo spires, a steep hike from the Bugaboo Provincial Park road
- [Elk Lakes Cabin](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/elk-lakes-cabin/) — in Elk Lakes Provincial Park, most often reached over Elk Pass from [Peter Lougheed](/accommodation/kananaskis-canmore) rather than from the BC side

Radium is also the southern gate to [Kootenay National Park](/accommodation/kootenay), and
Golden the link to [Yoho](/accommodation/yoho) and
[Rogers Pass](/accommodation/rogers-pass-selkirks).
