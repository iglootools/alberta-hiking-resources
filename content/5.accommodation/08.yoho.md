**Yoho National Park, BC** — [camping & booking](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/yoho/activ/camping/avantpays-frontcountry)

- **Reservable** — Kicking Horse, Takakkaw Falls (walk-in, tents only), Monarch *(FCFS in shoulder season)*
- **First-come, first-served** — Hoodoo Creek

::note
**Lake O'Hara** is a special case: no private vehicles, so you either hike the 11 km access road or ride the reserved Parks Canada shuttle bus. Both the day-use bus and the campground are allocated by advance reservation / random draw earlier in the year — see [Plan a trip to Lake O'Hara](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/yoho/activ/randonnee-hike/ohara/visit).
::

**Hostels**

- [HI Yoho / Whiskey Jack](https://hihostels.ca/en/destinations/hi-yoho-national-park) — temporarily closed for building upgrades; the nearest open hostels are in [Banff National Park](/accommodation/banff)

**Alpine Club of Canada huts**

Huts book through the [Alpine Club of Canada](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/huts/) on a rolling window — six months ahead for members, three for everyone else.

- [Lake O'Hara Hut](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/elizabeth-parker-hut/) (Elizabeth Parker) — at Lake O'Hara, and allocated by the same winter lottery as the campground
- [Little Yoho Valley Hut](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/hut/stanley-mitchell-hut/) (Stanley Mitchell) — Little Yoho, from Takakkaw Falls

The Wapta Icefield huts sit on this park's northern boundary but are reached from the
Icefields Parkway — see [Icefields Parkway and the Columbia Icefield](/accommodation/icefields-parkway).

These campgrounds are also the practical basecamp for Golden, which has none of its own — see
[Rogers Pass, the Selkirks, and Golden](/accommodation/rogers-pass-selkirks).
