The park is a long, narrow strip either side of Highway 93 South. Redstreak sits above Radium
at the southern end; the others are strung along the highway, an hour or more north.

**Kootenay National Park, BC** — [camping & booking](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/kootenay/activ/camping)

- **Reservable** — Redstreak (Radium, the largest and the only one with services), Marble Canyon, McLeod Meadows
- **First-come, first-served** — Crook's Meadow group camping and the smaller sites vary by season; confirm on the park's camping page, which is updated each spring.
- **Hostels and huts** — none in the park; neither Hostelling International nor the Alpine Club of Canada has a property here. The nearest are in [Banff National Park](/accommodation/banff), over Vermilion Pass.

Wildfire has repeatedly closed sections of this park and its campgrounds. Check the park page
for closures before planning around any one site.
