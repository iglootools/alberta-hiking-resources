The one region here you cannot drive to. Everything is a 27 km hike in from Mount Shark
(Kananaskis) or Sunshine (Banff), or a helicopter on scheduled flight days — so book the bed
before the trip, not the other way round.

**Mount Assiniboine Provincial Park, BC** — [park page](https://bcparks.ca/mount-assiniboine-park/)

- **Lake Magog campground** — the main backcountry campground, a short walk from the lodge. Permits are booked in advance through BC Parks; it fills for weekends through the summer.
- **Naiset Huts** — four historic cabins near Magog, with a shared cook shelter. Booked through [Mount Assiniboine Lodge](https://assiniboinelodge.com/), which manages them on behalf of BC Parks.
- **Mount Assiniboine Lodge** — [the lodge itself](https://assiniboinelodge.com/), catered and the only serviced accommodation in the park. Books up a season ahead.
- **Hind Hut** — an alpine hut below the mountain's north face, for climbing parties; see the [ACC hut network](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/huts/).

For the approach, the nearest road-accessible campgrounds are in
[Kananaskis Country and Canmore](/accommodation/kananaskis-canmore) at the Mount Shark end
and [Banff National Park](/accommodation/banff) at the Sunshine end.
