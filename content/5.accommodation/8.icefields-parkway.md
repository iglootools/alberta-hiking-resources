The Parkway runs 230 km with no town on it. Campgrounds and hostels are strung along its
length, roughly an hour apart, and most are basic — no services, and several are
first-come, first-served.

**Banff National Park (southern half)** — [camping & booking](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff/activ/camping)

- **Reservable** — Rampart Creek, Silverhorn Creek
- **First-come, first-served** — Mosquito Creek, Waterfowl Lakes

**Jasper National Park (northern half)** — [camping & booking](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/jasper/activ/passez-stay/camping)

- **First-come, first-served** — Wilcox, Columbia Icefield (tents) and the Icefields Centre RV lot, Jonas Creek, Honeymoon Lake, Mount Kerkeslin

- **Hostels** — [HI Mosquito Creek](https://hihostels.ca/en/destinations/hi-mosquito-creek), [HI Rampart Creek](https://hihostels.ca/en/destinations/hi-rampart-creek), [HI Hilda Creek](https://hihostels.ca/en/destinations/hi-hilda-creek) (group rental only, 8 km south of the Icefields Centre), and [HI Beauty Creek](https://hihostels.ca/en/destinations/alberta/hi-beauty-creek) north of the Icefield.

Either end has more choice: [Banff National Park](/accommodation/banff) to the south,
[Jasper and Mount Robson](/accommodation/jasper-robson) to the north. Saskatchewan River
Crossing, the one fuel stop on the highway, is also the gateway to
[David Thompson Country](/accommodation/david-thompson) on Highway 11.
